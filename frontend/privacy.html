Privacy and cookies

How this website handles information.

Effective August 18, 2026. This notice describes the website as it is currently implemented. It should be reviewed whenever forms, analytics, Google Sheets, Google Drive, or other services are added.

Who operates this website

ACE Outsourcing Solutions operates this website. Questions about prospective-client information may be sent to info@ace-outsourcing.com. Questions about careers or applicant information may be sent to hr@ace-outsourcing.com.

Information processed when you visit

Vercel hosts the public website and Render hosts the administrative API. Like most hosting services, they may process technical request data such as an IP address, browser and device information, requested URL, timestamps, and diagnostic or security logs. Their retention and use of provider logs are controlled by the Vercel Privacy Policy and Render Privacy Policy.

The website does not currently run analytics, advertising pixels, behavioral tracking, or marketing automation.

Contact and careers forms

The contact and careers forms are currently interface prototypes. Their contents and uploaded files are validated in your browser but are not transmitted to ACE, Vercel, Render, Google Sheets, or Google Drive. The information is discarded when the page is refreshed or the form is reset. Until a secure submission service is added, use the displayed ACE email address if you want to contact the company.

This notice must be updated before Google Sheets, Google Drive, email delivery, or another form-processing service is enabled.

The website does not store passwords, session identifiers, or other authentication credentials in localStorage or sessionStorage. The CSRF request token used by the admin interface is held only in page memory and disappears when the page closes or reloads.

No cookie banner is displayed because the current website does not set analytics, advertising, or other non-essential cookies. If that changes, the implementation and this notice must be reviewed before those technologies run.

Administrative security data

Admin login credentials are sent over HTTPS to the Render API. The configured password is stored by Render only as a salted scrypt hash. The API keeps a short-lived, in-memory record of an IP address and failed-login count for up to 15 minutes to limit repeated login attempts. The signed session cookie expires after no more than 8 hours.

Google Street View and external links

Google Street View is not loaded automatically. If you choose “Load Street View,” your browser connects directly to Google, which may receive technical data and information about your interaction under the Google Privacy Policy. Links to Google Maps, Facebook, and LinkedIn also take you to those services, where their policies apply.

Website content and photographs

The site displays employee photographs, workplace photographs, names, roles, quotations, client testimonials, and client logos. ACE is responsible for confirming that it has an appropriate legal basis, permission, release, or license for each item before publication. A private repository asset register flags items whose ownership or publication permission was not independently verified during development.

Retention and choices

Public website content remains available until an administrator changes or removes it. The current prototype forms do not retain submissions. Hosting and security-log retention is governed by the relevant provider. Depending on applicable law and the circumstances, you may ask ACE to review, correct, or remove personal information published on the site by contacting the relevant email address above.

Security and changes

ACE uses access controls, HTTPS, scoped secure cookies, CSRF protection, rate limiting, and security headers. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. This notice will be revised when the website’s actual data practices or providers change.
